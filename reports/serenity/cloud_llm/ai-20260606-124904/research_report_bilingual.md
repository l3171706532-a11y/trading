**中文 Thesis:** TSMC 仍然是 AI 芯片从设计走向量产的关键关口。随着高端 AI 加速器继续升级，领先制程与先进封装的产能、排产和交付节奏，决定了哪些客户能更快把算力产品真正送上货架。它不是 AI 需求本身，而是 AI 需求能否兑现为出货和收入的核心供给节点。

**English Thesis:** TSMC remains a key gate from AI chip design to mass production. As high-end AI accelerators continue to upgrade, leading-edge wafer capacity, advanced packaging capacity, scheduling, and delivery cadence determine which customers can actually convert AI demand into shipped products and revenue. It is not the demand engine itself; it is a core supply node that decides whether AI demand can be translated into output.

Supply-chain position: AI accelerator design -> leading-edge foundry -> advanced packaging (including CoWoS-class capacity) -> assembly/test -> hyperscaler deployment. TSMC sits at the foundry and advanced-packaging bottleneck layer, where process complexity, customer qualification, and capacity reservation can create scarcity.

Risks:

- Advanced packaging capacity could expand faster than AI demand, easing the scarcity premium.
- Customers could diversify more production away from TSMC if alternative manufacturing or packaging routes become viable.
- Export controls, regional demand shifts, or geopolitics could distort bookings and visibility.
- If AI demand slows while capex keeps expanding, the bottleneck thesis could weaken.
- A strong revenue story is not enough if the constraint is no longer tight; the market may already be pricing the obvious demand exposure.

Falsification plan:

- If TSMC discloses materially looser advanced-packaging utilization, shorter lead times, or clearly non-binding capacity, downgrade the chokepoint thesis.
- If major AI customers demonstrate meaningful manufacturing diversification away from TSMC at scale, reduce the control-point rating.
- If capex guidance rises but AI-linked backlog or reservation evidence does not tighten, treat the expansion as supply normalization rather than scarcity.
- If order visibility, margin mix, or customer commentary show that AI demand is no longer constrained by TSMC capacity, the thesis should be marked as weakened.
