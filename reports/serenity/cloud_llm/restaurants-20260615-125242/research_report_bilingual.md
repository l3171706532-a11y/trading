**English Thesis:** Hypothesis: DIN's key variable is not merely one quarter of same-restaurant sales at Applebee's, IHOP, or Fuzzy's. The real chokepoint is whether franchisee-level cash returns are strong enough to support remodels, advertising contributions, development commitments, lower closure pressure, and dual-brand expansion. If franchisee economics improve, DIN's asset-light royalty stream, advertising system, proprietary product sales, and development pipeline could prove more durable than a mature casual-dining screen implies.
