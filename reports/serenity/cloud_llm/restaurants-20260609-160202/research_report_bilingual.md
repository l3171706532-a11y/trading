- Competitive risk: broadline distributors, cash-and-carry wholesalers, local specialty distributors and direct supplier relationships can limit CHEF's pricing power.
- Supply-chain and regulatory risk: food safety issues, recalls, supplier disruptions, ingredient availability, labeling rules and independent certifications can affect product availability and reputation.
