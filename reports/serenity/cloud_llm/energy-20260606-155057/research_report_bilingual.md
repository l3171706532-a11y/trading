**English Thesis:** Constellation is one of the cleanest deep-research targets for the scarce firm carbon-free power chokepoint. AI data centers require reliable, long-duration, auditable electricity, and hyperscaler demand for 24/7 clean power may reprice existing nuclear assets as scarce infrastructure rather than generic generation. Nuclear assets are difficult to replicate because of permitting timelines, operating complexity, capital intensity, and regulatory constraints. The key factual evidence is Constellation's announced 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft to support the Crane Clean Energy Center and restart Three Mile Island Unit 1. CEG is therefore a core test case for whether AI data-center demand is willing to pay a premium for firm clean power.

Supply-chain position: Demand driver: AI/cloud data centers requiring reliable 24/7 power. System layer: hyperscaler electricity procurement. Scarcity mechanism: hard-to-replicate nuclear assets, long licensing and restart timelines, high operational requirements, limited replacement supply.
